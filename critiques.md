# Critiques of six constructed practice samples

**Read this first.** All six images were drawn by me in code (SVG, rendered to PNG). They are practice samples. They are **not** AI-generated, and I am **not** evaluating any vendor's or model's real output. I built three to be strong and three to be plausible but flawed, so I know the "answers" in advance. That makes this a demonstration of *how I write a critique*, not a test of my judgment against unknowns. The scores come from one rater (me) and have not been checked against anyone else.

Method: `rubric.md`. Scores are 1 to 5. `[C]` = convergent (broadly agreed craft). `[D]` = divergent (direction and taste). Briefs below are ones I made up for each sample so the scoring has something to anchor to.

## 1. Dusk lighthouse (`samples/01-strong-dusk-lighthouse.png`)

**Brief:** Flat-vector poster of a lighthouse at dusk. Calm, quietly hopeful. Landscape.

| Comp | Hier | Color | Cohes | Craft | Mood | Orig | Cult | Mean |
|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|
| 4 | 5 | 4 | 4 | 4 | 4 | 3 | N/A | 4.0 |

**What works**
- The lighthouse sits on the right third and the setting sun on the left third. The beam links them, so the eye travels across the frame instead of stopping on one object.
- Hierarchy is clear. The lantern is the lightest value in the image and sits against the darkest sky, so it wins in the first glance. The sun is second. The birds are a quiet third.
- The tower is shaded warm on its left and cool on its right, which agrees with a sun low on the left. The sun's reflection narrows toward the bottom, as it should.
- Dark rock shapes frame the bottom corners, which keeps the glow in the middle of the picture.

**What fails**
1. `[C]` The sun and the lantern are close in brightness. They compete a little. The beam is also brighter than the sunset sky behind it, which would not happen at this light level. It reads as a graphic device, not as light.
2. `[C]` Light logic on the rock. The small foreground rock on the left has a warm rim along its top edge. The large cliff under the tower has none, though it faces the same sun. The large cliff looks flat and unlit by comparison.
3. `[C?]` The three birds sit right on the beam's edge. They are small and read as specks of noise instead of a deliberate detail.

**Fix**
- Reduce the beam's opacity by about a third, or shrink the sun by about 20%, so the lantern clearly leads.
- Add a 2 px warm rim along the cliff's left-facing edge, matching the foreground rock.
- Move the birds about 60 px up, off the beam edge.

**Convergent vs. divergent**
- `[C]` the hierarchy near-tie, the missing rim light.
- `[D]` the flat-vector-with-grain direction; warm sunset over cool blue hour; the "hopeful" reading. A reviewer who wanted a colder, lonelier mood would score Mood differently and would not be wrong.
- `[D]` Originality 3: lighthouse at dusk is one of the most common poster subjects, so I did not give more.

## 2. Warm still life (`samples/02-strong-warm-still-life.png`)

**Brief:** Quiet, minimal still life of ceramics in soft window light. Warm, calm, editorial.

| Comp | Hier | Color | Cohes | Craft | Mood | Orig | Cult | Mean |
|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|
| 4 | 4 | 4 | 4 | 4 | 4 | 2 | N/A | 3.7 |

**What works**
- Three heights (tall bottle, wide bowl, small cup) step down from left to right. The rhythm leads the eye along the table. The window light on the right balances the cluster on the left, and the empty wall in between gives the group room.
- Color is controlled. The ground is a warm neutral, terracotta is the only large saturated mass (so it is the clear primary), sage is secondary, and a small yellow is the accent.
- Highlights sit on the left of each object and shadows fall to the right. They agree with each other.

**What fails**
1. `[C]` The small yellow object is orphaned. It reads as a ball or an egg, and its scale is unclear. Its shadow is a small flat ellipse, unlike the long soft shadows the other objects cast.
2. `[C]` The light patch on the wall has sharp window panes, but nothing on the table carries those panes, and the patch sits to the right while the objects' highlights suggest light from the left. It is close to consistent but not fully resolved.
3. `[C?]` The bottom third of the frame is a large, even tan table with no variation. It feels like dead space instead of intentional quiet.
4. `[C?]` The curved line on the bowl reads as a seam, so it is unclear whether the bowl has a lid.

**Fix**
- Enlarge the yellow object by about 30%, give it the same long soft shadow as the others, or remove it.
- Add a soft pane-shaped light shape on the table that lines up with the wall patch.
- Crop about 10% off the bottom, or add a very faint gradient to the table.

**Convergent vs. divergent**
- `[C]` the orphan accent and its shadow, the light-patch logic.
- `[D]` Originality 2. Soft ceramics in window light is a very familiar editorial look. Another reviewer could reasonably call the restraint a distinct voice and score it 3 or 4. I would want to see one unexpected choice (material, scale, framing) before going higher.

## 3. Geometric poster (`samples/03-strong-geometric-poster.png`)

**Brief:** Bauhaus-inspired geometric poster. Confident, balanced, print-like. Portrait.

| Comp | Hier | Color | Cohes | Craft | Mood | Orig | Cult | Mean |
|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|
| 4 | 4 | 5 | 4 | 4 | 4 | 2 | 3 | 3.8 |

**What works**
- The large red disc is clearly the primary. The tall navy column is second and overlaps the disc, which creates tension and a sense of depth.
- Yellow (half disc) and teal (quarter disc) balance each other on the diagonal. A single baseline rule organizes the lower third.
- The palette is four colors plus paper, with clear value separation: navy is darkest, yellow lightest. It also reads in grayscale.
- The type is one family, tracked capitals, aligned to the same margin as the shapes. A faint grain keeps it from looking sterile.

**What fails**
1. `[C]` Tangent. The outermost ring inside the red disc has a radius of 200, which brings it exactly to the edge of the navy column. Two lines meet at a point, which creates a visual "pinch".
2. `[C?]` The small navy dash under the disc reads as a stray mark, not as an intentional element.
3. `[C]` The teal quarter disc crosses the baseline rule and sits right next to the caption. It pulls weight to the bottom-right corner and crowds the type.
4. `[C?]` The ring hairlines are low contrast. At thumbnail size they turn into a muddy texture on the red.

**Fix**
- Change the ring radius from 200 to about 190 so it stops short of the column.
- Delete the dash, or repeat it as three dashes with a rhythm so it looks deliberate.
- Move the teal shape about 40 px right, or bleed it off the edge, and leave a clear gap above the caption.
- Raise ring opacity to about 80% or thicken the lines to 3 px.

**Convergent vs. divergent**
- `[C]` the tangent, the crowded corner.
- `[D]` Originality 2: Bauhaus-revival geometry is a very common look.
- Cultural relevance 3. The vocabulary of early-20th-century modernism (primary-leaning palette, asymmetric balance, sans-serif capitals) is used correctly, but as a generic "modern design" shorthand, without any specific new context. `[C]` that it is accurate; `[D]` how much that counts.
- `[D]` whether the grain helps or fights the flat, print-like intent.

## 4. Muddy fruit bowl (`samples/04-flawed-muddy-fruit-bowl.png`)

**Brief:** Warm, inviting still life: a bowl of fruit in soft afternoon light.

| Comp | Hier | Color | Cohes | Craft | Mood | Orig | Cult | Mean |
|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|
| 3 | 2 | 1 | 3 | 3 | 2 | 2 | N/A | 2.3 |

**What works**
- The structure is sound: a centered bowl, grounded by a cast shadow, with readable silhouettes and clean edges. No artifacts.

**What fails**
1. `[C]` Color and value have collapsed. Every area is a dirty olive-brown within a narrow band of mid-dark values. In grayscale the wall, the bowl and the fruit are nearly the same gray. There is no clean accent, so I cannot tell what the fruit is.
2. `[C]` Hierarchy. The bowl leads only by its shape. The fruit does not separate from the bowl interior, and the eye has nowhere to land.
3. `[C]` Lighting. There is no readable light source. The only highlight is a faint rim, and the shadow is a flat gray ellipse. The "soft afternoon light" in the brief is not delivered.
4. `[C?]` The drapes on the left and right read as dark slabs, not as cloth. The cup at the right edge sits nearly on the frame edge with no purpose.

**Fix**
- Widen the value range: lift the wall to a warm mid-light, darken the bowl interior and the table front.
- Give the fruit two clear color families (for example a warm red-orange and one clean green) at higher saturation, and keep the wall low in chroma so the fruit is the only color.
- Pick one light direction: a single clear highlight, and a warm shadow that is tinted, not gray.
- Remove the cup, and lighten or remove the drapes.

**Convergent vs. divergent**
- `[C]` value collapse, ambiguity, no light logic.
- `[D]` A deliberately dark, low-key still life is a valid direction. But "low-key" keeps a clear value pattern with a few bright notes. This image has no bright notes, so it is muddy, not moody. That distinction is the convergent part.

## 5. Crowded night market (`samples/05-flawed-crowded-night-market.png`)

**Brief:** A lively city night market. The viewer should feel the buzz but still find one main point of interest.

| Comp | Hier | Color | Cohes | Craft | Mood | Orig | Cult | Mean |
|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|
| 2 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 2 | 2 | 2 | N/A | 2.0 |

**What works**
- The style is consistent (flat, frontal). A shared purple base unifies the palette. The dark crowd silhouettes along the bottom add a front layer of depth. The repetition does communicate "lots going on".

**What fails**
1. `[C]` Hierarchy has collapsed. There are 36 lanterns, 96 windows, 6 signs, 6 stalls, 6 vendors and 3 moons, all at the same size, saturation and glow. Nothing is primary. The brief asked for one main point of interest.
2. `[C]` Composition is a rigid six-column grid with no entry point or route. The three equal moons read like a traffic light.
3. `[C]` Lighting logic. Everything glows, but nothing is lit by anything. No light falls on the awnings, the ground or the people. The crowd stays flat navy.
4. `[C]` Craft. Lanterns overlap the signs and windows, which hides the signs and creates cluttered collisions.
5. `[C]` Color. Six neon hues at the same saturation and value vibrate against each other, with no dominant hue.

**Fix**
- Choose one hero stall at about the left third. Give it a warm orange light pool on the ground, and rim-light the nearest crowd figures.
- Darken and desaturate the other five stalls by about 40%.
- Cut the lanterns by two thirds and run them as one strand that leads to the hero stall.
- Keep one moon at about 60% size, or remove them.
- Make about 70% of the windows dark, and vary stall widths.

**Convergent vs. divergent**
- `[C]` all five problems above.
- `[D]` A maximalist, saturated direction can be valid. Even then, a maximal image needs one dominant element and a lighting logic. Density alone is not the problem. Equal density everywhere is.

## 6. Cabin lighting logic (`samples/06-flawed-cabin-lighting-logic.png`) and revision (`samples/06b-revision-cabin-lighting-logic.png`)

**Brief:** Calm dusk at a lake cabin. Warm, safe, quiet.

| | Comp | Hier | Color | Cohes | Craft | Mood | Orig | Cult | Mean |
|---|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|
| Before (06) | 4 | 3 | 4 | **2** | 3 | 4 | 2 | N/A | 3.1 |
| After (06b) | 4 | 4 | 4 | **4** | 3 | 4 | 2 | N/A | 3.6 |

**What works**
- The cabin sits on the right third and the sun on the upper-left third. Together they make a diagonal balance, and the open water leaves quiet space. The mountain facets and the trees are lit on the sun side. The palette is a harmonious warm-to-violet range, and the calm mood comes through.

**What fails (before)**
1. `[C]` The sun is on the left, but the cabin's brightest wall is the right-facing one, and the left wall is in shade. The trees and mountains are lit on their left, so the cabin disagrees with the rest of the scene.
2. `[C]` The cabin's cast shadow stretches left, toward the sun. The tree shadows fall right.
3. `[C]` The sun's reflection sits about 235 px to the right of the sun. A reflection should lie directly below it.
4. `[C]` The window glow is nearly as strong as daylight, so it reads as a second main light. It lights nothing around it. Together with the large bright sun, it pulls attention from the cabin, which is the story.
5. `[C?]` The shadows are hard-edged polygons that do not attach cleanly to the base of each object, and the two trees are the same stamped shape.

**What I changed (06b)**
- Swapped the cabin's wall and roof values so the left faces are lit and the right faces are in shade.
- Flipped the cabin shadow to fall right, like the tree shadows.
- Moved the reflection directly under the sun.
- Reduced the window halo from about 80% to about 35%.

**Result:** Cohesion 2 to 4. Hierarchy 3 to 4, because the lit wall now agrees with the sun and the window no longer competes with it. Mean 3.1 to 3.6.

**Still weak after revision**
- The shadows are still hard-edged and slightly detached. Both trees are still the same shape. The sun is still the strongest value in the frame, so the cabin is a clear second, not first. I scored Craft 3 in both versions because I did not fix that. Originality stays 2.

**Convergent vs. divergent**
- `[C]` every item in the fail list. This is the clearest convergent case in the set: the image implies one light source and then contradicts it.
- `[D]` Whether the cabin should be the brightest thing, or whether letting the sun lead is the right call for this mood.
